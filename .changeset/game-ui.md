'the-fighting-game': minor

- The game has a UI with a shop screen and a battle screen.
- Hovering a card lifts it and shows its description, rarity and prints.
- Battles show a health strip with a countdown and a timer, and energy icons
  that explain themselves on hover.
- Cards shake when they trigger, and damage flies to the target's health in the
  color of its type.
