- Runs open with a draft of 5 abilities, and offer another pick every 8
  phases. There are 22 abilities, each named after an animal.
- Abilities trigger on a cooldown and combine two aspects. The shop favors
  cards that share those aspects.
- Speed shortens ability cooldowns and Slow lengthens them.
- Bosses come with abilities.
- Runs are now endless and only end when the last life is lost.
