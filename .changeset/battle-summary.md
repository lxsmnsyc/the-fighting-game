'the-fighting-game': minor

- A battle summary opens when a battle ends. It shows the result, the gold
  earned and each side's stats. Continue moves on to the next shop.
- Card rarities unlock in order. Picking an ability unlocks Common cards, 10
  Common cards unlock Uncommon ones, and 10 Uncommon cards unlock Rare ones.
  Secret cards no longer show up in the first shop.
- Replaying a round after a loss rolls a new shop instead of the same offers.
