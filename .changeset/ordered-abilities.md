- Added 22 abilities, for 44 in total.
- The order of an ability's aspects now matters. The first aspect is what the
  ability draws on, and the second is what it produces.
- Each new ability takes the aspects of an existing one in reverse order. For
  example, Crab (Attack, Armor) gains Armor from Attack, while Rhino (Armor,
  Attack) attacks harder with Armor.
- Viper, Scorpion, Octopus, Cheetah, Mantis and Badger swapped their aspect
  order to match what they do.
