---
'the-fighting-game': minor
---

- Battles are fought between alliances of teams, so a round can have more than
  two units.
- Units pick a random standing enemy for attacks, magic and enemy energy.
- Cards run only while enabled, and stop when disabled or removed.
- Armor, Dodge and Corrosion consume the defender's energy instead of the
  attacker's.
- Healing no longer raises health above the maximum.
- Small amounts of consumable energy now run out instead of lingering.
- Aspect cards add their own energy instead of always adding Speed.
- Dual Wield and Double Attack no longer trigger on their own repeated attacks.
- Endure reduces damage the unit takes instead of damage it deals, and its
  delayed damage ticks every second.
- Card values from Error prints are now seeded.
