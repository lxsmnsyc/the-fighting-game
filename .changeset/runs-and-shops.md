---
'the-fighting-game': minor
---

- A run starts from a random seed with 3 lives, and is won by clearing 8 phases.
- Each phase has 3 rounds, and the last one is a boss.
- Each round opens a shop of 5 cards that can be bought or rerolled, then a
  battle against a generated opponent.
- A win or a draw moves to the next round. A loss costs a life and replays the
  round.
- Battles that run past 60 seconds end in a draw.
- A run can be saved and resumed at the start of any round. Each round's shop
  and opponent come from the seed and the round number.
